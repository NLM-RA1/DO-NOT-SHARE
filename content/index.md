This website was made by one of Dr Neelima's research assistants to aid students (you people) in quickly getting a grasp on using Excalidraw to make concept maps. You should already had obsidian and the Excalidraw plugin installed (the web version of Excalidraw would work too but not recommended)

While browsing this guide, keep in mind that **there are many other ways to achieve the same outcome**, making a concept map is quite like drawing after all. 

For those who find using Excalidraw intuitive and do not need a guide, I still highly recommend checking out the **INTERACTIVE CM** and **Stylization** section
